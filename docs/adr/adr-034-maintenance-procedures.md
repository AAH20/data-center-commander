# ADR-034: Maintenance Procedures

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Maintenance work must follow defined procedures with hazards, entry/exit criteria, and required roles.

## Decision
Procedures capture: procedure key, version, title, domain, risk class, required roles, hazards, entry criteria, steps, exit criteria, approval policy, and status. Procedures link to work orders and workflows. Versioned procedures ensure work follows the approved method. Hazards and exit criteria ensure safety.

## Consequences
- **Positive:** Standardized maintenance; safety awareness; clear roles and criteria.
- **Negative:** Procedure maintenance overhead; requires regular review.
- **Mitigation:** Procedure versioning; risk class assessment; approval policies.
